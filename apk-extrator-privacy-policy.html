APK Extractor Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how APK Extractor, provided by Dodemy (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Information the app accesses

APK Extractor requires broad package visibility to perform its core function. The app reads metadata for applications installed on your device, including application name, package name, version, requested permissions, installation classification, APK file location, and application icon.

This installed-application inventory is processed locally on your device. We do not upload or sell your installed-app list.

2. APK extraction and sharing

When you choose to extract an APK, APK Extractor copies the selected package file into app-controlled storage on your device. When you choose to share an extracted APK, Android grants the receiving application temporary, read-only access through a secure FileProvider URI.

We do not receive the extracted APK. You control where it is stored and which application or person receives it. You are responsible for complying with applicable licenses, intellectual-property rights, and laws when extracting or sharing software.

3. Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, approximate location derived from an IP address, ad interactions, and related data to provide, secure, measure, and improve advertising.

Google’s handling of data is governed by its own policies. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

4. Remove Ads purchases

Remove Ads purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. The app receives purchase status and entitlement information from Google Play and stores a local preference indicating whether ads should be removed.

Google Play transactions are subject to the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Permissions

  • Query all packages: lists installed applications for browsing, searching, inspecting, and extracting APK files.
  • Internet and network state: loads advertisements, communicates with Google Play Billing, and opens requested web resources.
  • Billing: offers and restores the optional Remove Ads purchase.

6. Data retention

Installed-app metadata is loaded into memory while the app is running. Extracted APKs remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall APK Extractor. Theme and ad-removal preferences remain in local app storage until app data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Advertising and purchase providers may retain information according to their own privacy and retention policies.

7. Data security

We use Android platform protections, app-private storage, read-only content sharing, and encrypted network connections provided by our service providers. No method of electronic processing is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children’s privacy

APK Extractor is a utility application and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Your choices

You may stop all application processing by closing or uninstalling APK Extractor. You can delete extracted files using your device’s file-management controls, reset local preferences by clearing app data, and purchase Remove Ads to disable advertisements in the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality, legal requirements, or service-provider practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest revision.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Dodemy at dodemyinc@gmail.com.
