Data Deletion Request for DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL

Last updated: July 8, 2026

App and Developer Details

This page explains how users of DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL can request deletion of data sent to the developer.

App name: DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL
Developer / legal entity: Maduabughichi Achilefu (Dodemy Inc)
Deletion request contact: dodemyinc@gmail.com

Account Status

DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL does not allow users to create an account and does not maintain an app account database. Because there are no user accounts, there is no in-app account profile to delete.

Data That Can Be Deleted

Users may voluntarily send emails to report a bug, contact us, or contact the developer. If you sent such an email, you may request deletion of the support, contact, or bug-report information that we can reasonably identify from your request.

This may include:

  • Your email address as shown in the message you sent.
  • The subject and body of your support, contact, or bug-report email.
  • Any optional device details included in the email, such as Android version, SDK version, or device model.
  • Any other information you voluntarily included in the email.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to dodemyinc@gmail.com with the subject line DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL Data Deletion Request.

Include enough information for us to locate the message you want deleted:

  • The email address you used when contacting us.
  • The approximate date you sent the message.
  • The subject line of the message, if you remember it.
  • A short statement that you want your DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL support/contact data deleted.

Retention and Processing Time

We will review deletion requests and delete identifiable support, contact, or bug-report information within a reasonable period after verifying and locating the relevant message. Some information may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, abuse-prevention, dispute-resolution, or compliance reasons.

Data Not Controlled by Us

DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL uses Google Mobile Ads SDK services to show ads. Advertising-related data processed by Google is controlled by Google and its applicable privacy settings. Users can manage Android advertising personalization and related privacy controls in their device settings and Google account settings where available.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL handles data, review our Privacy Policy.
