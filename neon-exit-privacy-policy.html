01 · Accountability

Who is responsible for this policy?

Neon Exit is provided by Maduabughichi Achilefu (Dodemy Inc). In this policy, “Dodemy,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to that provider. This policy applies to the Neon Exit Android application and the legal pages linked from it.

If you use an ad, Google Play, an email app or another third-party service from Neon Exit, that service may have its own privacy notice and controller. This policy explains Dodemy's handling and does not replace those third-party notices.

02 · Data map

What data does Neon Exit handle?

The exact data flow depends on the features enabled in the build you install. The release design is offline-first: the level pack is bundled with the app, and the normal single-player experience does not require a Dodemy account or game server.

Game and settings data

Progress, level results, streaks, daily-challenge results, sessions, settings, accessibility choices, sound preferences and in-app wallet state are stored in the app's private Android storage so the game can resume and work offline.

Optional player identity

The leagues feature can create a random, pseudonymous install identifier and an assigned display name. If you choose a custom name, that name and your scores can be used for the leaderboard. No email address is required.

Country display

A country code used for a league flag may be inferred on-device from SIM/network information or the device locale. Neon Exit does not request precise location permission and does not geolocate you.

Purchases and consent

Google Play processes payment details. Neon Exit receives purchase entitlement state, such as whether ads are removed, and the advertising consent state needed to decide whether an ad may be requested. We do not receive your card number.

Advertising data

If the free experience displays ads, Google Mobile Ads may process device, advertising, network, approximate-location and ad-interaction information under Google's policies and your consent choices. Dodemy does not control Google's independent processing.

Support information

If you select “Write us,” the email composer may be pre-filled with the app version, device model, Android version and the anonymous install identifier. You can review and remove that information before sending it.

Data Neon Exit does not request

  • contacts, address books, call history, SMS or calendar data;
  • camera, microphone, photos or broad file-library access;
  • precise GPS location or background location;
  • an account password, date of birth or identity document; or
  • an Android ID, IMEI or advertising ID as the game's own player identifier.

03 · Purpose

How we use information

We use the information described above only as needed to:

  • run the game, save your progress and remember your preferences;
  • provide optional daily challenges, league standings and name availability checks;
  • deliver, measure and cap advertising where ads are enabled and permitted;
  • complete, restore and secure in-app purchases through Google Play;
  • respond to support requests that you initiate;
  • protect the app, detect failures and maintain service reliability; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce the Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the local game state to build an advertising profile for Dodemy. Google may process information for its own services according to the notices linked in Services and sharing.

04 · Providers

Services and sharing

Neon Exit shares information only with the providers needed for a feature you use, and with a configured league service when that optional online feature is enabled. These providers may process data on servers outside your country.

  • Google Play: distributes the app and processes purchases, refunds, account eligibility and purchase restoration. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform: request and display ads, manage consent and report ad delivery or interaction. Read Google's publisher and app privacy guidance and Google's privacy policy for the applicable details.
  • Optional league service: if a build is configured with a live league API, it may receive the random install identifier, display name, country code, weekly score and name-availability requests. The default simulated league is on-device and sends none of this data.
  • Your chosen apps: when you open a browser, Play Store or email composer, Android hands control to that app. Its processing is governed by its provider and your relationship with it.

We may disclose information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal process, or to protect users, Dodemy and the security of the app. We do not disclose local game files to other apps as part of normal gameplay.

05 · Lifecycle

Retention, storage and deletion

Local game data remains in the app's private storage until you reset it through the app, clear Neon Exit's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Android backups and device migration are controlled by your device and account settings; do not rely on a backup as a permanent archive of progress.

If online leagues are enabled, the optional service may retain the pseudonymous league record for as long as needed to operate seasons, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Because the record is not tied to an email account, send a deletion request to dodemyinc@gmail.com with enough information to identify the install record. Do not include passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Google controls retention for Google Play, Google Mobile Ads and its consent tools. Use the controls provided by Google or contact Google for requests about data held directly by those services.

06 · Control

Your choices and rights

  • Play offline: the bundled puzzle experience can be used without enabling a live league service or sending game data to a Dodemy server.
  • Manage advertising consent: open Settings → Privacy options when the control is available. You can also use the consent controls provided by Google.
  • Review before sending: the support email is user-initiated; edit or delete pre-filled diagnostics before sending.
  • Delete local data: reset progress in the app or clear the app's storage/uninstall it through Android. Clearing storage also removes locally stored preferences and wallet state.
  • Request help: email us if you want to ask about access, deletion, correction or a privacy concern. We may need to verify the request before acting.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under privacy law, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. We will handle valid requests subject to applicable law and the limits imposed by third-party services.

07 · Safeguards

Security

Neon Exit uses Android private storage for local state, random install identifiers rather than hardware identifiers, and HTTPS for configured network services. The app also validates persisted state and is designed to fall back to local gameplay when an optional service is unavailable.

No software or transmission is perfectly secure. Keep Android updated, install the app from a trusted source, protect your device, and avoid sending information by email that you would not want included in a support ticket.

08 · Age

Children's privacy

Neon Exit is not directed to children under 13, and you must meet the minimum age required to use digital services in your country. We do not knowingly collect a child's personal information. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

If a change is material, we will provide additional notice when required by law. Continuing to use Neon Exit after an updated policy takes effect means the updated policy applies, subject to any rights that cannot legally be waived.
