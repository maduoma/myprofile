Overview

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL accesses, collects, uses, shares, retains, and deletes when you use the app. The app is designed primarily to provide Bible study content and related learning materials for Anglican Communion of Nigeria Sunday school study. The word "Adult" in the app name refers to the Sunday school study category; it does not mean the app contains sexually explicit, mature, or age-restricted adult content.

Information We Collect

DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, payment details, contact lists, photos, videos, precise location, or government identification numbers.

The app may process or transmit the following information in limited situations:

  • User-initiated email information: If you choose to send a bug report, contact us, or contact the developer through the app, the app may open your email client with a message that you can review, edit, send, or discard. The email may include information such as your Android version, SDK version, device model, and any details you type into the email. This information is visible to you before you send it. Sending the email is completely optional. If you do not send the email, we do not receive it.
  • Advertising information: The app includes the Android Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and uses Google Mobile Ads SDK services to show ads. Google and its partners may process device information, Advertising ID, approximate location inferred from network data, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and other information described in Google's advertising and privacy documentation.

How We Use Information

We use information only for purposes reasonably expected by users of DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL:

  • To review and respond to support requests, developer contact messages, or bug reports that you voluntarily send.
  • To troubleshoot app compatibility issues across Android versions and device models.
  • To display, measure, limit, and improve ads served through Google Mobile Ads SDK services.
  • To comply with legal, security, fraud-prevention, and Google Play policy requirements.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal and sensitive user data. We may share or allow processing of information only in the following situations:

  • With Google advertising services: Google Mobile Ads SDK services may receive and process advertising-related data to provide ads, ad personalization where permitted, ad measurement, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and service improvement.
  • With email providers: If you voluntarily send us a support, developer contact, or bug report email, the email is processed by the email services used to deliver and receive the message.
  • For legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when necessary to protect users, the app, or the public from security, fraud, or abuse risks.

Third-Party Services

DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL uses third-party services and software components that may process data according to their own privacy practices:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google advertising services: Google Privacy Policy
  • AndroidX and Google Material Design Components are used to build the app interface and do not independently receive personal information from us.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information handled through the app. Data transmitted to third-party services is handled according to those services' security practices. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL does not create user accounts and does not maintain an app account database. Bug report, support, and developer contact emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate, respond to, resolve, document, and prevent the reported issue, unless a longer retention period is required for legal, security, or compliance reasons.

You may request deletion of support information that you previously sent to us by emailing dodemyinc@gmail.com. Include enough detail for us to locate the message, such as the email address you used, the approximate date, and the subject of the request. You can also review the dedicated Data Deletion Request page.

Your Choices

  • You can choose not to send bug reports, support emails, or developer contact emails.
  • You can review, edit, or discard any email before sending it.
  • You can manage Android advertising personalization choices in your device settings where available.
  • You can contact us to ask privacy questions or request deletion of support information.

Children's Privacy

DNDN ADULT SUNDAY SCHOOL is a Bible study app that can be used by people of different ages and does not include age-restricted adult content. We do not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a voluntary email or other contact, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.
