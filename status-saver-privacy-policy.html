01 · Foundation

Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies to Status Saver - Status Downloader for Android (the “App”), published under the package name com.dodemy.statussaver_statusdownloader.

The App is provided by Maduabughichi Achilefu (Dodemy Inc) (“Dodemy,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). For information handled directly by the App, Dodemy is the responsible developer. Google and other third parties described below process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. The App’s use is also governed by our Terms of Service.

02 · Summary

At a glance

No Dodemy account or media server

The App does not require you to create an account. Status discovery, preview, saving, and the saved-media index run on your device.

You control folder access

On supported Android versions, you choose a media folder through Android’s system folder picker. The App retains read access only after you grant it.

Google services process limited data

The free experience may use Google Mobile Ads and Google’s consent platform. Optional purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

You can remove local data

Clear the App’s storage or uninstall it to remove its local database and preferences. Files you saved to your gallery remain until you delete them.

03 · On-device data

Information used locally on your device

The following information is accessed or stored to provide App features. It is not uploaded to a Dodemy-operated server.

Status media and folder access

When you select the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business media folder, the App receives a system-issued folder URI and persistent read permission. It scans image and video files in the selected status folders so you can preview, filter, save, share, or repost them. A copy is created in your device’s media collection only when you save an item or enable Auto Save.

Saved-media index

A local Room database records the saved file URI, source URI, file name, MIME type, whether the item is a video, and the saved timestamp. This index powers the Saved screen and helps prevent duplicate saves.

Preferences and entitlement

Android DataStore stores your selected language, onboarding completion, Auto Save setting, notification setting, share-link setting, selected media folder URI, and whether an eligible remove-ads purchase is active.

Direct Chat

A phone number and optional message entered in Direct Chat remain in temporary on-screen state while you use that screen. The App formats them into a wa.me link and sends that link to WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, or another app you choose only when you initiate the action. Dodemy does not store or receive the number or message.

Local diagnostics

Development builds may write feature events and error details to the device’s Android debug log for testing. This logger is disabled in production builds and does not transmit logs to Dodemy.

05 · Purpose

How information is used

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

  • locate and display status media you have authorized the App to read;
  • save, index, find, share, repost, and delete media at your direction;
  • remember settings and maintain optional Auto Save and notification features;
  • create a Direct Chat link and hand it to the app you select;
  • show ads in the free experience, subject to applicable consent controls;
  • offer, verify, acknowledge, and restore the remove-ads purchase;
  • protect ad and purchase systems against fraud and abuse; and
  • diagnose issues during development without running Dodemy analytics in production.
06 · Android access

App permissions

Photos, videos, and storage

Used to discover and save status media. Depending on your Android version, the App uses Android’s system folder picker or legacy storage permissions limited to older Android versions.

Notifications

Used to show optional local notifications for App activity, including Auto Save results. Android asks for this permission on supported versions, and you may deny or revoke it.

Internet and network state

Used by Google Mobile Ads, consent management, Google Play Billing, and opening web content you request. Status media is not uploaded to Dodemy.

07 · Disclosure

Sharing and disclosure

Dodemy does not sell your status media, Direct Chat phone number, or Direct Chat message. Information may be disclosed only in these circumstances:

  • At your direction: to WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, or another app when you open, share, or repost content.
  • To service providers: Google processes the advertising, consent, and billing data described above under its own terms.
  • For legal and safety reasons: if reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce legal rights, or protect users and the public. Because Dodemy does not receive your local media or Direct Chat input, we generally cannot disclose content we do not possess.
  • Business changes: information under our control may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and this policy.
08 · Lifecycle

Retention and deletion

App records

The local saved-media index and preferences remain on your device until you remove individual saved items where supported, clear the App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. These records are excluded from the App’s Android cloud backup and device-transfer configuration.

Saved files

Media copied to your device’s gallery remains there until you delete it from the App, your gallery, or a file manager. Clearing App storage or uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete those media copies.

Folder access

You can connect a different folder from the App. You can also remove the App’s storage access through Android system settings or by clearing App storage.

Provider records

Google and apps you choose may retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. Requests concerning those records should be directed to the relevant provider. You may also email Dodemy for help identifying the appropriate control.

09 · Control

Your choices and rights

Depending on your device, region, and applicable law, you can:

  • decline, revoke, or reconnect media-folder access;
  • disable Auto Save, notifications, or share-link behavior in App settings;
  • manage notification permission in Android settings;
  • use Google’s consent form when presented to manage eligible ad choices;
  • purchase the optional remove-ads product or continue using the ad-supported version;
  • delete saved media and local App data as described above; and
  • request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability where those rights apply to information controlled by Dodemy.

Because the App has no Dodemy account and does not upload your status media or Direct Chat input to us, Dodemy cannot retrieve or delete those local items remotely. We may need reasonable information to verify and respond to a privacy request, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights.

10 · Safeguards

Security

The App uses Android-scoped content access, read-only persistent folder grants, URI-based sharing, release-disabled diagnostic logging, and backup exclusions for its local database and preferences. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device, screen lock, Google account, WhatsApp account, and saved media. Please install updates from trusted stores and do not grant access to folders you do not want the App to read.

11 · Global services

International processing

Google and other third-party providers may process information on servers located outside your country. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards they use. Dodemy’s own App functionality keeps the media and local records described in Section 3 on your device.

12 · Age

Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their country. We do not knowingly ask a child to provide personal information to Dodemy. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

A parent or guardian should supervise a minor’s use of the App and ensure that any saving, sharing, or reposting of media is lawful and respectful of others’ privacy.
