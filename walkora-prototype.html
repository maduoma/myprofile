Things to try

  • Onboarding: tap “Not now” on the permission dialog to see the denial-recovery path (ONB-03).
  • Guest → account: open Community as a guest, sign in contextually, watch history migrate (J2). Then accept Dele's friend request from the 🔔 bell.
  • Practice board: as a guest, race the labelled virtual pacers on Today — medals, live updates, adjustable pace (LDB-05).
  • Fair ranking: switch the leaderboard between Goal % / Steps / Consistency / Improved — ranks re-sort (LDB-02).
  • Compare: tap anyone on a board — days won, win streaks, and the daily-winner strip. Cheer 🎉 or Nudge 👉; no Taunt, by design.
  • Groups: Community → Groups → create “Morning Crew”, watch a member join, then open the group chat (GRP/MSG).
  • Celebrate: complete the goal — confetti respects reduced-motion.
  • Share: Today → Share — privacy preview with redaction toggles (ACH-03).
  • Chat safety: ⋮ in a conversation → mute, report, block (MSG-02).
  • Theming: Profile → Appearance — app theme is independent of this page.

About

Single-file prototype of the Walkora PRD/tech-spec. All data is simulated and stored locally in your browser; steps tick while Today is open. Companion docs: walkora-prd.html · walkora-tech-spec.html
